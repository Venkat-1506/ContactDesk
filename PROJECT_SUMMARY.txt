                      PEOPLE ATLAS - PROJECT OVERVIEW
========================================================================

WHAT IS PEOPLE ATLAS?
---------------------
People Atlas is a personal visual network explorer and archival directory 
designed to map, organize, and explore the people in your life.

Instead of a generic address book or SaaS database, People Atlas represents 
your contacts as an interactive personal network portrait, visual map, and digital field notebook.


WHAT DOES YOUR PROJECT DO AS OF NOW?
------------------------------------

1. NETWORK INSIGHTS (ANALYTICS REPORT PAGE)
   - Title: "NETWORK INSIGHTS"
   - Subtitle: "Understand your network through the patterns in your people, places, and relationships."
   - Navbar primary link: "INSIGHTS" (routing `/insights` and `/atlas`).
   - Dynamic Editorial Analytics & Personal Network Report calculated 100% live from localStorage contact data.
   - Top Minimalist Information Line: 100 PEOPLE / 5 GROUPS / 15 PLACES / 20 CLOSE.
   - Dynamic Network Snapshot: "Your contact network spans 5 groups and 15 places."
   - Contacts by Group: Vertical bar chart showing real category distribution.
   - Network Composition: SVG Donut chart with category percentages and hover legend.
   - Where Your People Are: Horizontal bar chart of top locations.
   - Relationship Mix: Frequency distribution bar chart of relationship types.
   - People You Keep Close: Close circle share percentage & indicator bar.
   - Contact Data Health: Completeness percentages for Phone, Email, Location, Organization, Relationship, Notes.
   - What Stands Out: 3-5 dynamically calculated, rule-based observations.
   - Network Balance: Classification matrix for Group Diversity, Place Diversity, Close Share, and Data Health.

2. SINGLE AUTHORITATIVE "ADD PERSON" ACTION
   - Top Navbar is the single, authoritative place for the primary "+ Add Person" action.

3. PEOPLE DIRECTORY (VIEW ALL CONTACTS)
   - Complete directory satisfying all contact management requirements.
   - Multi-field live search across 8 attributes (`⌕ Find someone...`).
   - Keyboard shortcut: Pressing "/" key instantly focuses search input.
   - Alphabetical section headers (A to Z) with archival Monogram initial boxes.
   - Swipeable text navigation tabs with active underline for mobile and desktop.
   - Mobile contact rows: Call (`tel:`) & Email (`mailto:`) actions on left, 
     with Heart (Favorite) and `>` Chevron icon strictly anchored at the far right end!

4. PEOPLE I KEEP CLOSE (FAVORITES PAGE WITH SEARCH)
   - One-touch star/favorite toggle to maintain an inner-circle directory.
   - Live Search Bar on Favorites page (`⌕ Find in close people...`) with `/` shortcut.
   - Mobile contact rows with Heart & `>` Chevron right-aligned at the far right end.

5. PLACES EXPLORER
   - Explore your personal network grouped by location/city 
     (e.g., Chennai, Vellore, Madurai, Bengaluru).
   - Sectional typographic headers with contact counts and direct profile links.

6. RECENT ACTIVITY & CONTACT TRACKER
   - Timeline activity journal for RECENTLY CONTACTED, RECENTLY ADDED, and RECENTLY UPDATED.
   - "Mark as Contacted Today" button updating interaction timestamps.
   - Relative time calculations (e.g. "Just now", "2 days ago", "14 days ago").

7. PERSON RECORD (CONTACT DOSSIER)
   - Full personal profile view featuring initial Monograms, relationship 
     badges, phone call links (tel:), email links (mailto:), and 
     one-click copy phone/email buttons.
   - Complete history timestamps (Created, Last Updated, Last Contacted).
   - Add/Edit/Delete actions with safety confirmation dialogs.

8. DATA BACKUP, IMPORT & PRIVACY
   - 100% Local Storage: Data is stored safely in your browser (localStorage). 
     No cloud server or login required.
   - Export & Import JSON backups.
   - Reset sample data or clear storage with safety prompts.

9. ENHANCED ARCHIVAL STYLING & ACCESSIBILITY
   - Rich paper texture background gradient (`--bg-texture`).
   - Dark ink typography (`#23201C`), warm terracotta (`#7A3E2B`), forest green (`#263F3A`), 
     and archival slate (`#3B4B59`) accents.
   - Fully responsive layout for desktop, tablet, and mobile screens without horizontal scroll.
   - 100% local SVG icons.


TECHNOLOGY STACK:
-----------------
- React 19 (User Interface)
- React Router v7 (Navigation & Routing)
- Vite (Build Tool & Dev Server)
- HTML5 / Vanilla CSS (Editorial System)
- LocalStorage API (Browser Persistence)
